<h2>Updating your abstracts</h2>
<p>All authors are requested to update their abstract in line with the reviewers' recommendations, and resubmit to the conference <a href="https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=healtac2026" style="color:#2f7d95;">Easychair</a> site.</p>

<h2>Complete the authors' details form</h2>
<p>Please complete this <a href="https://forms.cloud.microsoft/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=FM9wg_MWFky4PHJAcWVDVsbd6Y26XndIhZAf3BOno2JUNldLMU1NTE5YUkcwOTY0N1k5TFFNWENQOC4u&route=shorturl" style="color:#2f7d95;">short form</a> (less than 1 minute) if you have not already done so, so that we can include your abstract in the final programme and consider a full version of your paper for inclusion in a <a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health" style="color:#2f7d95;">Frontiers in Digital Health</a> special issue.</p>

<h2>Lightning talks and PhD / fellowship talks</h2>
<p>All lightning talks and all PhD / fellowship talks are accepted for a poster session in addition to an oral session, and are requested to prepare both a lightning talk and a poster, as notified in your acceptance email.</p>

<h2>Presentation format</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Please prepare your talks as either PDF or PowerPoint.</li>
  <li>You are requested to keep to time, and will be stopped by the chair if you go over time.</li>
  <li>Please limit the number of slides you present accordingly.</li>
  <li>We suggest 10 slides for a full paper, and 2 slides for a lightning talk.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Presentation upload</h2>
<ul>
  <li>All talks will be presented from a central computer.</li>
  <li>Speakers cannot use their own laptop.</li>
  <li>Presentations must be uploaded at the registration desk or by email to <a href="mailto:healtac2026@kcl.ac.uk" style="color:#2f7d95;">healtac2026@kcl.ac.uk</a> by 5pm the day before the session in which you will talk.</li>
  <li>Please name your presentation as follows: <code>nn_surname.pdf</code> or <code>nn_surname.pptx</code>, where:
    <ul>
      <li><code>nn</code> is your submission number</li>
      <li><code>surname</code> is the first author's surname</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>

<h2>Posters</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Format: A1 landscape or A1 portrait.</li>
  <li>Note that A0 posters will not fit the poster boards and will be excluded from poster sessions.</li>
  <li>Although we will have some Velcro dots for fixing posters to boards, we suggest you bring your own if you can, so that you can affix your poster without delay.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Demos</h2>
<ul>
  <li>All demo authors will be provided with a table plus a board for an A1 poster (landscape or portrait).</li>
  <li>Demonstrators must provide their own screens and computer equipment.</li>
  <li>The venue (Holiday Inn Brighton Seafront) is responsible for Wi-Fi and Internet connection, not the conference organisers.</li>
  <li>HealTAC 2026 cannot guarantee any particular level of Wi-Fi / Internet service.</li>
  <li>If an internet connection is important for your demo, we suggest you provide your own mobile-based connection.</li>
</ul>
